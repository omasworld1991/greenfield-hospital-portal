Greenfield Hospital Services

Our Medical Services

At Greenfield Hospital, we are committed to providing high-quality healthcare services tailored to meet the needs of individuals and families. Our experienced healthcare professionals use modern equipment and evidence-based practices to deliver safe, compassionate, and patient-centered care.

General Consultation

Our general outpatient clinic offers comprehensive medical consultations for children, adults, and the elderly. Whether you require a routine check-up or treatment for an illness, our physicians are ready to provide expert medical care.

Emergency Services

Medical emergencies require immediate attention. Our emergency department operates 24 hours a day, providing rapid assessment, stabilization, and treatment for life-threatening conditions and urgent medical needs.

Surgical Services

Our surgical department performs both minor and major procedures using modern surgical techniques and equipment. Every procedure is carried out in a safe, sterile environment by experienced surgeons and perioperative nurses.

Surgical services Include

  • General Surgery
  • Orthopaedic Surgery
  • Obstetrics & Gynaecological Surgery
  • Laparoscopic Surgery
  • Emergency Surgery

Diagonistic Services

Accurate diagnosis is the foundation of effective treatment. Our diagnostic department provides a wide range of laboratory and imaging services to support quick and reliable medical decisions.

Available Diagonistic Tests

  • Medical Laboratory
  • Digital X-Ray
  • Ultrasound Scan
  • CT Scan
  • Electrocardiogram (ECG)
  • Blood Chemistry
  • Urinalysis

Maternity and Women's Health

Our maternity unit provides comprehensive care throughout pregnancy, childbirth, and the postnatal period. We are committed to ensuring the health and safety of both mother and baby.

Services Include

  • Antenatal Care
  • Safe Delivery
  • Caesarean Section
  • Family Planning
  • Postnatal Care

Paediatric Care

Our paediatric specialists provide preventive and curative healthcare services for infants, children, and adolescents in a friendly and comfortable environment.

Pharmacy Services

Our licensed pharmacy ensures that patients receive genuine medications, professional counseling, and guidance on proper drug usage.

Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation

We help patients recover mobility, strength, and independence following injury, surgery, or chronic medical conditions through personalized rehabilitation programs.

Why Choose Greenfield Hospital?

  • Experienced Medical Professionals
  • Modern Medical Equipment
  • 24/7 Emergency Care
  • Patient-Centered Healthcare
  • Affordable Healthcare Services
  • Clean and Safe Environment
